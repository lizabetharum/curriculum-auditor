Before you start
What happens to your files. Your lesson and any student work are sent to Claude, Anthropic's AI model, to be read. Nothing is saved on this site. Your results stay in this browser window until you download them. Claude can be wrong, so treat every result as a first draft for a teacher to check.
Step 1

Upload a lesson

Word (.docx) works best: the lesson is split at its headings. PDFs are split by page. Markdown and plain text also work. Up to 4 MB.

Step 2

Check the lesson

Step 3, optional

Score student work

Pick one task from the lesson. Claude scores each response on the skills that task gives students a chance to practice, with a level from 1 to 4, or "too little to score" when the work does not show enough. Each response is scored three times, and the most common result is shown. When the runs disagree, the result is flagged for a teacher to look at.
